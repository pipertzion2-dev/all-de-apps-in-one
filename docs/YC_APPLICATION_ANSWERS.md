## What is your company going to make? Please describe your product and what it does or will do.

```
zzai zzai (zzaizzai.com) leads with Poor Man Protection: creators upload sketches, designs, code, or media; we seal them with SHA-256 hashes, platform timestamps, and a custody trail, then export a structured court-ready evidence pack plus a public verify link. It is supporting evidence of anteriority/possession — not a USPTO patent or Copyright Office registration. Klean Sneaks is the free demo of the same “protect what you made” idea; Seeds, Orbit, Hybrid², and other desk tools ship in beta around that core.
```

## Why did you pick this idea to work on? Do you have domain expertise in this area? How do you know people need what you’re making?

```
Every creative fight starts with “who had it first.” Formal registration is slow and expensive; most people ship unprotected. I’m a full-stack builder and parent designing with my son’s eye for brand — we need a cheap, defensible prior-art trail for sketches and product ideas. Domain: cryptographic hashing, custody logs, product packaging, and shipping creator tools live. Demand shows up as people asking for seals, verify links, and court packs before they ask for more AI features.
```

## Who are your competitors? What do you understand about your business that they don't?

```
Competitors: Copyright.gov / USPTO (slow, formal), DocuSign-style e-sign, blockchain “timestamp NFT” toys, and generic file lockers (Dropbox, Drive). Law firms for affidavits.

What we get that they don’t: one productized workflow — hash seal + independent-style timestamp token + custody metadata + public verify + court PDF — aimed at creators before counsel, not after. We’re not selling “register a patent”; we’re selling immediate evidentiary packaging with a brand and demo people actually use.
```
